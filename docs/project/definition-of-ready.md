# Definition of Ready (DoR)

An issue is **Ready** when the team can start it without guessing at its purpose or expected result.

## Checklist

- [ ] The objective and problem/context are clear.
- [ ] Scope is bounded; important exclusions are stated where needed.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria describe observable outcomes.
- [ ] Related requirement, epic, or issue is linked when applicable.
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are identified.
- [ ] Responsible area and accountable owner are identified, or the assignment rule is clear.
- [ ] Relevant design/API/data constraints are documented or linked.
- [ ] The task is small enough to estimate and review; otherwise, it is split.
- [ ] Required test or verification evidence is described.

If an item is not applicable, state why rather than leaving a critical ambiguity. A task may remain in **Backlog** until these conditions are met; move it to **Ready** only when the checklist is satisfied.
